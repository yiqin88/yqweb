— Day 01 / The drive up

Singapore to KL.

Early start to beat the Causeway. Two charging stops, family lunch at A'Famosa, into Hilton by mid-afternoon.

Day 1 Tue, 23 June

The long drive

≈ 360 km
  1. 5:30am
    Depart Woodlands

    Everyone in the car, last toilet check, fuel/charge to 100% the night before. Snacks + water + passports + VEP ready.

    Tip: Weekday 5:30 AM = the best Causeway window. Aim to clear immigration by 6:15 AM.
  2. 7:30am
    Yong Peng Lay-by rest stop

    First proper break ~140 km in. Toilet, breakfast — roti canai, nasi lemak, kopi-O. Stretch legs, kids run around the open area.

    Stay: 30–45 min Charging: not Tesla-confirmed — verify on PlugShare before relying on it
  3. 9:00am
    Freeport A'Famosa Outlet Tesla Supercharger

    250 km mark. Tesla Supercharger (DC fast) — ~40 min to 80%. Mall is bright and quiet at this hour. Coffee + early lunch at the food court so you arrive Hilton ready to rest, not hungry.

    Open in Maps ↗
    Charge: 40 min Early lunch: 60 min
  4. 11:30am
    Depart A'Famosa for KL

    ~130 km via PLUS Highway. Kids likely nap. Aim to arrive Hilton ~1 PM.

  5. 1:00pm
    Check in: Hilton Kuala Lumpur early check-in

    Hilton KL confirmed all 3 requests (same floor + adjacent rooms, early check-in, rooms away from lift lobby) — see "Before the Trip" section. If rooms ready: settle in, parents/kids nap until 3:30 PM. If not: leave bags, light snack at Vasco's, room by 3 PM standard.

    Open in Maps ↗
    Conf: 3471664730 Tel: +60 3 2264 2264 Honors: 2773814641
  6. 1:30pm
    Rest in rooms important

    Everyone has been up since 4:30 AM. Two hours of horizontal time saves the day. Parents will sleep, kids will play, you will check the Tesla is on the valet charger.

  7. 4:00pm
    KLCC Park & Petronas Twin Towers kid favourite

    15-min Grab from Hilton (~RM 12). View the towers from the park (better than the skybridge for first-timers). Playground for Charlotte, gardens for parents to sit. Coffee + stroll until dinner.

    Open in Maps ↗
  8. 7:00pm
    Dinner: Madam Kwan's at Pavilion KL

    Famous KL Malaysian — Nasi Lemak Tut Tut, Asam Laksa, Nasi Bojari. Broad menu, all kid-friendly, no beef issues. Reservation recommended for 6 pax (call +60 3 2148 2240).

    Open in Maps ↗
    Budget: ~RM 250–350 for 6 pax
  9. 8:30pm
    Lake Symphony fountain show

    Stroll back to KLCC park after dinner. Fountain shows every hour from 8 PM until 10 PM — 15 min each, gorgeous against the lit towers. Then back to Hilton for sleep.

If KLCC feels too far Easier alternative: walk from Hilton to NU Sentral mall (covered walkway, 5 min). Dinner at Madam Kwan's NU Sentral branch instead. Less iconic but zero taxis, parents-friendly.
— Day 02 / Climb into cool air

KL morning, Awana afternoon.

Light KL morning, climb up to cool air by mid-afternoon. Settle at Awana, rest by the pool, dinner down at Gohtong Jaya. Tomorrow is the big theme-park day.

Day 2 Wed, 24 June

From hot KL to cool hills

≈ 50 km
  1. 7:30am
    Hilton breakfast

    Honors breakfast may be included — confirm at check-in. Strong coffee for parents, pancakes for kids. Don't linger past 9 AM.

  2. 9:30am
    Check out, bags to concierge

    Standard checkout 12 noon — checking out now frees up the morning. Leave luggage with concierge, walk to KLCC area (10 min).

  3. 10:00am
    Aquaria KLCC kid favourite

    Right at opening time — quieter. Underground aquarium beneath KLCC. 90 minutes is enough. Parents can sit while kids ogle stingrays in the tunnel.

    Open in Maps ↗
    Tickets: ~RM 75 adult / RM 65 child (book online for 10% off)
  4. 11:30am
    Lunch: Restoran Yut Kee early lunch

    Hainanese-Malaysian kopitiam since 1928. Roti babi, Hainanese chicken chop, kaya toast. Iconic. Going at 11:30 avoids the lunch rush — tight space, but easier seating for 6 pax. Cash preferred.

    Open in Maps ↗
    Budget: ~RM 150 for 6 pax Beef-free: yes
  5. 1:00pm
    Pick up car from Hilton, drive to Awana winding road

    ~50 km via Karak Highway. 1 hour at sensible pace. The climb is twisty — kids prone to carsickness should sit front-passenger or take Bonamine 30 min before.

  6. 2:00pm
    Check in: Resorts World Awana early check-in

    Mid-station, cool 18–22°C. Both Superior Deluxe rooms, 2 Queen beds each. Early check-in request sent ~1 week before (see "Before the Trip"). If rooms ready: settle by 2:30 PM. If not: bags to bellman, late lunch at hotel cafe.

    Open in Maps ↗
    Ref: EL0000100460 Pkg: Mid-Year Hotel Escape
  7. 3:00pm
    Awana afternoon: pool, walk, settle easy

    Awana has a heated outdoor pool — perfect at 20°C. Kids in the water, parents in cafe chairs reading. Or walk the resort grounds and viewpoints. No cable car today — save the round-trip for tomorrow when you have the rest base.

  8. 6:30pm
    Dinner: Loong Kee Gohtong Jaya

    Local Chinese tze char at the foot of the mountain — 15 min drive down from Awana. Generous portions, no-frills authentic. Parents will appreciate proper hawker-style Chinese over resort food. Order claypot dishes, sambal prawns, kang kong belacan, fried tofu.

    Open in Maps ↗
    Drive: 15 min down from Awana Budget: ~RM 200–300 for 6
  9. 8:30pm
    Back to Awana, sleep rest up

    Tomorrow is the big theme-park day. Make sure phones are charged, theme-park tickets ready (Klook QR in the new Tickets section), Tesla parked, kids in bed by 10.

You have a Day 2 First World room (10693258AL) Originally booked on wrong dates — you've kept it rather than cancel (the SGD 33 is non-refundable anyway). Use cases: (a) anyone who feels off after the climb can nap at the peak instead of Awana; (b) optional cable car up Day 2 evening for SkyAvenue dinner, room as rest base, then cable car down — but this burns your one round-trip ticket. If you don't want to use it, no harm — the room just sits there, you sleep at Awana as planned, and you've already paid.
— Day 03 / Theme park day

SkyWorlds, full send.

The big one. Cable car up, full day at SkyWorlds, First World room (booking 10693538AL) as the daytime rest base. Cable car down after dinner at peak.

Day 3 Thu, 25 June

Rollercoasters & rest

Theme park
  1. 8:00am
    Breakfast at The Lodge use meal credit

    The Awana buffet. Your booking includes RM 18 × 2 meal credit. Rates: RM 35 adult / RM 15 child. ~RM 134 out of pocket for 4 adults + 2 kids after credit. Fuel up — long day ahead.

  2. 9:30am
    Awana SkyWay cable car UP round-trip starts

    10–15 min ride. Glass-bottomed gondolas optional. Skip Chin Swee stop (Day 4). This is your ONE round-trip cable car for the trip — return portion gets used tonight after dinner. Pack a day-bag (change of clothes, kids meds, water, snacks).

    Fare: ~RM 10–15 round trip per person Pack: day-bag only, leave main luggage at Awana
  3. 10:00am
    SkyAvenue → SkyWorlds entry check weather

    Theme park typically opens 11 AM weekday. Use the 30-45 min before opening to find the park entrance, queue early for the popular rides. Check the Genting app the night before — outdoor rides close in heavy fog or rain. If forecast is bad, swap Day 3 with Day 4 plan (Chin Swee + Skytropolis).

  4. 11:00am
    Genting SkyWorlds — morning session main event

    Malaysia's newest outdoor theme park. Charlotte (8) may be under the 130 cm bar for the wildest coasters — Nicholas (11) should clear most. Hit the headliners first while queues are short. Meeting point + a "grandparents' shaded bench" at central plaza.

    Tickets: ~RM 200 adult, RM 170 child (book online, sometimes 20% off)
  5. 12:30pm
    Lunch inside the park

    Food courts and themed restaurants throughout. Eating off-peak (1:30 PM onwards) skips queues. Park food is meh but convenient — saves the energy of leaving and re-entering.

  6. 2:00pm
    Check in First World, rest at the room use the booking

    Walk from SkyWorlds to First World Hotel (2 min, right next door). Check in to booking 10693538AL under Hui Shan's name. Ask reception for late checkout to 3 PM tomorrow when you check in — extends the room's usefulness. Parents nap, kids can chill or watch TV. You finally sit.

  7. 4:00pm
    SkyWorlds — afternoon session round 2

    Back to the park for the rides you missed. Parents can stay at First World if they're done for the day. The remaining 2.5 hours is plenty to clear the kid-friendly rides Charlotte couldn't do in the morning rush.

  8. 6:30pm
    Park closes, collect parents from First World

    Pick up day-bag, regroup with the grandparents. Walk to SkyAvenue for dinner.

  9. 7:00pm
    Dinner at SkyAvenue (peak)

    Quick eat after a long day. Sushi Zanmai for kids (lots of non-beef options), Imperial Rama for Thai. Not the special dinner of the trip — that's tomorrow at Genting Palace.

    Budget: ~RM 250–350 for 6
  10. 8:30pm
    Cable car DOWN round-trip complete

    Last ride down at ~11 PM but no point staying that late — everyone's exhausted. Cable car back to Awana, sleep. Tomorrow is the slower drive-up day.

First World note — daytime use only Per Option Z, the First World room is daytime-only — no one sleeps there tonight. The overnight portion of booking 10693538AL goes unused (~SGD 33 already paid). That's a deliberate trade-off: simpler logistics, everyone sleeps at Awana, no rollaway or split family. The room's real value is the 2-4 PM rest break for grandparents during the theme-park grind.
— Day 04 / Temple, hot pot & treat

Drive up, charge up.

Mountain temple, hot pot lunch, drive up to the peak (Tesla charges while you're at Skytropolis), then the treat dinner at Genting Palace.

Day 4 Fri, 26 June

Chin Swee to Crockfords

Drive up day
  1. 8:00am
    Breakfast: Gohtong Jaya local kopitiam

    Drive 15 min down for authentic Chinese kopitiam breakfast — kopi-O, kaya toast, soft-boiled eggs, wan tan mee, fish ball noodle. Highly-rated local options: Restoran Loke Yun (4.6★), Wan Loi (4.5★), or the corner stalls at Pasar Pagi Gohtong Jaya.

    Open in Maps ↗
    Drive: 15 min down from Awana Budget: ~RM 100–150 for 6 Cash-friendly
  2. 10:00am
    Chin Swee Caves Temple parents will love

    Drive up from Gohtong Jaya (~15 min, parking available). Sprawling Taoist-Buddhist temple complex on the cliffside. 9-storey pagoda, Buddha statue, prayer halls, panoramic mountain views. Moderate walking with stairs — lifts are available between major levels. 2 hours is plenty.

    Entry: free Donations: appreciated
  3. 12:30pm
    Lunch: Sangong Charcoal Hot Pot at Gohtong Jaya

    Mongolian-style charcoal hot pot — drive back down 15 min to Gohtong Jaya. Hot pot in 20°C weather is exactly right. Order half-half spicy & clear broth, mix of fresh seafood, sliced meats (skip beef for parents), mushrooms, tofu, leafy veg. Kids love throwing things in.

    Open in Maps ↗
    Drive: 15 min down from Awana Budget: ~RM 350–500 for 6
  4. 2:00pm
    Drive UP to the peak Tesla charges here

    From Gohtong Jaya, ~25 min drive up the Genting access road to the top. Park near the Hilltop EV chargers (opposite the rebranded RW Hotel, formerly Highlands Hotel). Plug in immediately upon arrival — you'll be at the peak for 5+ hours so even a slow charger fills the battery comfortably.

    Drive: 25 min up Charge time at peak: 5+ hours available
  5. 2:30pm
    Skytropolis Indoor Theme Park all ages

    Gentle indoor rides — perfect for Charlotte and grandparents to enjoy together. Carousel, mini drop tower, kiddie coaster, bumper cars. Inside First World so no weather worries. 2 hours covers most of it. (Genting Strawberry Farm is permanently closed.)

    Tickets: ~RM 60–80 each, day passes available
  6. 5:00pm
    Browse SkyAvenue, coffee break

    Window shop the luxury arcade, take photos of the indoor "sky" projection ceiling, grab coffee. Check on the Tesla — likely full by now. Parents can sit, kids can wander supervised.

  7. 7:00pm
    Special dinner: Genting Palace at Crockfords treat night

    Premium Cantonese at Crockfords. Walking distance from the Hilltop carpark. Menu shows "per portion" 每份 — that means per dish to share family-style, not per person. For 6 pax, order 5-6 dishes: e.g. Steamed Tooth Fish (RM 250, feeds 4-5), Sweet & Sour Prawn (RM 98), XO Scallops with Broccoli (RM 72), one chicken, vegetable, soup, rice. Reservation essential — call concierge first day.

    Total: ~RM 600–900 for 6 (one nice dinner) Beef-free: easy
  8. 9:00pm
    Drive DOWN to Awana night driving

    Tesla fully charged from the afternoon. Drive carefully — the Genting road can be wet at night, fog possible in lower stretches. ~25 min back to Awana. Kids will sleep in the car.

Pack for tomorrow tonight Saturday is the long drive home with the worst Causeway day of the week. Pack the car the night before so you're not faffing at 9 AM. Make sure Tesla is parked at Awana ready to roll.
— Day 05 / Home

Genting back to Singapore.

Saturday Causeway is the busiest of the week. Leave early, charge at A'Famosa over lunch, target SG by sunset.

Day 5 Sat, 27 June

The long road home

≈ 400 km
  1. 7:30am
    Quick coffee at Awana lobby

    Light only — coffee + pastry. Save your appetite for the proper Yong Peng breakfast en route. Bags packed from last night, kids dressed, ready to roll.

  2. 9:30am
    Check out, load car, depart

    Aim to be on the Karak Highway by 10 AM. Tesla should be at 90%+ from overnight charging at Hilltop. Descent regenerates a few %.

  3. 11:30am
    Yong Peng: proper breakfast 4.5★ local

    Same lay-by area as the way up, now for a real meal. Yong Peng is known for Chinese kuey teow, chicken noodles, fish ball noodles. Highly-rated: Tian Tian Lai Wan Tan Mee (4.6★) or Restoran Lai Lai (4.5★). Use the bathroom break to stretch legs and check Tesla range.

    Open in Maps ↗
    Stay: 45 min Budget: ~RM 100–150 for 6
  4. 12:30pm
    Freeport A'Famosa: charge stop Tesla Supercharger

    Same Supercharger as Day 1. Already had Yong Peng breakfast — just charge + coffee here. 40 min charge, then back on the road. Kids can stretch in the mall briefly.

    Open in Maps ↗
    Charge: 40 min Total stop: ~60 min
  5. 2:30pm
    Continue south

    ~250 km to go. Stops only as needed.

  6. 5:30pm
    Approach Causeway Saturday traffic

    Saturday evening = worst Causeway window. Plan for 1–1.5 hours at immigration. Tuas usually less crowded than Woodlands for return — consider routing via Tuas Second Link. Have passports out, snacks accessible, kids' bladder schedule respected.

  7. 7:30pm
    Home in Singapore

    Unpack tomorrow. Order in. Everyone collapses.

Alternative timing If you're flexible: leave Awana at 5 AM Saturday and target Causeway by 11 AM — clears in 30 min usually. But waking sleepy kids at 4:30 AM after a busy week is a hard sell. The 9:30 AM departure is the right compromise.

— EV Charging

Tesla game plan.

Two confirmed Supercharger stops. Two destination chargers. Range is comfortable if you charge before leaving.

  • Pre-trip: Charge to 100% at home night before

    Don't leave it to the morning. Full battery + 6 pax + AC + luggage = real range ~400 km.

  • Stop 1: Yong Peng lay-by ~140 km in

    Primarily a food + toilet stop. Tesla charging not confirmed here — check PlugShare app before relying on it. Worst case: skip charging here, go direct to A'Famosa.

    Purpose: rest, breakfast

  • Stop 2: Freeport A'Famosa Outlet ~250 km in

    Confirmed Tesla Supercharger (DC fast). 40 min to 80%. Easy mall to wait at. Restrooms, food, kids' shops.

    Connector: CCS2 / Tesla Speed: up to 150 kW

  • Stop 3: Hilton KL valet parking ~360 km / overnight

    Tesla Destination Chargers in valet parking. Slower (~7 kW) but you'll be there 12+ hours. Tell valet at check-in.

    Connector: Type 2 Cost: usually free for guests

  • Stop 4: Genting Hilltop chargers Day 4 — 26 Jun

    Confirmed by RW Awana. Drive up Day 4 afternoon (from Gohtong Jaya after Sangong lunch). Park near the chargers, plug in immediately, then walk to Skytropolis. 5+ hours at the peak means even a slow charger fills the battery comfortably while you're at dinner.

    Access: drive up via Genting access road Located: opposite RW Hotel (formerly Highlands Hotel)

  • Return: Freeport A'Famosa again 27 Jun lunch

    Same Supercharger, same 40-min routine. Pair with lunch. Should arrive Singapore with 30%+ buffer.

— Must-eat list

What to eat where.

A short list, not a long one. All beef-free-friendly. Kids' menus where it matters.

In KL

Madam Kwan's

Must

Iconic Malaysian. Nasi Lemak Tut Tut, Asam Laksa, Nasi Bojari. Broad enough for kids and parents both.

Where: Pavilion KL or NU Sentral · Booking: call ahead for 6 pax

Restoran Yut Kee

Must

Hainanese kopitiam since 1928. Roti babi, chicken chop with brown sauce, kaya butter toast. Bring cash. Service is brisk.

Where: Jalan Kamunting · Closed: Mondays

Lot 10 Hutong

Food court

Curated hawker stalls inside a basement food court. Try Hokkien mee, char kway teow, hai nan chicken rice. No need to wander Petaling Street with the family.

Where: Lot 10, Bukit Bintang · Air-conditioned

Bijan Bar & Restaurant

Date night

If you and wife want one nice evening without the kids (leave them with grandparents at Hilton). Modern Malaysian, refined plating. Tasting menu available.

Where: Off Jalan Ceylon · Reservation: essential

Roti Canai (any kopitiam)

Breakfast

The Malaysian breakfast essential. Flaky flatbread, dal, curry. Available everywhere. Kids' version: plain with kaya. ~RM 3 each.

Where: any old kopitiam, Yong Peng lay-by has these

Durian — Musang King

Seasonal · June peak

If parents and you are durian people: June is peak Musang King season. SS2 morning market (Petaling Jaya) has stall after stall. Eat on-site, can't bring back to hotel.

Where: SS2 Morning Market, Petaling Jaya · Best: 8–10 AM

At Genting

Genting Palace

Treat night

Cantonese fine dining at Resorts World. Dim sum lunch is famous; dinner does the works — Peking duck, steamed fish, abalone. The right spot for the special dinner with parents. Reserve via concierge.

Where: Resorts World, Genting · Budget: RM 500–800 for 6

The Olive

Mediterranean

Mediterranean comfort food. Calm setting, varied menu, easy for everyone. Good "I just want a quiet dinner" option.

Where: Resorts World · Booking: recommended

Imperial Rama

Thai

Solid Thai at SkyAvenue. Tom yum, pad thai, green curry. Kids do well with the milder dishes.

Where: SkyAvenue, top station

Coliseum Cafe

Local

Old-school Malaysian-Western at Genting. Sizzling steaks, chicken chop, classic kopitiam. Atmospheric, the kids will find it interesting.

Where: First World · Note: menu has beef — order non-beef for parents

Hot pot / steamboat

Cool weather

Perfect at 18°C. Multiple hot pot places at First World and SkyAvenue. Kids love picking ingredients. Get a half-half clear/spicy soup.

Where: First World food courts

Mushroom Farm Restaurant 香菇园饭店

Loong Kee Gohtong Jaya

Sangong Charcoal Hot Pot 三公炭火锅

Loke Yun · Wan Loi (Gohtong Jaya)

— Practical stuff

Things to get right.

🛂Border crossing

  • VEP (Vehicle Entry Permit) required for SG cars in Malaysia since Oct 2024. Confirm yours is active.
  • Touch 'n Go card needed for tolls — top up before leaving SG.
  • Outbound: Tue 5:30 AM Woodlands → clear by 6:15 AM ✓
  • Return: Sat afternoon = busy. Consider Tuas Second Link instead of Woodlands.
  • Passports valid 6+ months. Kids' IC documents.

💵Money & cards

  • Bring some RM cash — small stalls, parking, durian.
  • Cards work most places. DBS Multiplier/Live Fresh good for foreign use.
  • Yut Kee is cash only.
  • Rough rate: SGD 1 ≈ RM 3.5 (check before leaving).
  • ATMs at A'Famosa, Hilton, Resorts World.

☔Weather

  • KL: 28–34°C, humid. Afternoon thunderstorms possible.
  • Genting: 15–22°C — bring warm layers for everyone, especially parents.
  • Genting fog: outdoor rides can close. Check Genting app the night before.
  • June is monsoon-light but pack a small umbrella each.

📱Phone & data

  • Singtel ReadyRoam ~SGD 11/day per line — easiest.
  • Or local SIM: Hotlink/Celcom tourist plans at Causeway.
  • Download PlugShare (charging), Waze (Malaysia traffic), Genting World (theme park).
  • WhatsApp works the same — no setup needed.

  • AA Malaysia (breakdown): 1-800-88-0808
  • Hilton KL: +60 3 2264 2264
  • RW Awana: +60 3 2718 1118
