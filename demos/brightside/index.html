— 02 / Our approach

Small classes. Real teaching.

  1. 01
    Same teacher every week

    Your child sees the same MOE-trained teacher all term. No rotating tutors, no inconsistent grading.

  2. 02
    Max 6 students per class

    Every child gets called on. Every question gets answered. Big enough for discussion, small enough to be noticed.

  3. 03
    Weekly parent reports

    You get a one-paragraph email every Sunday: what we covered, how your child did, what to practise.

  4. 04
    No tedious homework dumps

    Targeted practice. Quality over quantity. We don't fill the week with worksheets that don't change anything.
